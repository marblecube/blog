Adamantite
by Vidal Palacios

The stream rippling against the rocks is Gaia sharing secret messages with me and me alone.

But then at dusk Ouranos spreads himself
thin and starlit, 
a ceiling of old fire

And he is also here, low and moving,
the water I mistook for her voice.

This is how he touches her without descending
runs cold over stone for centuries,
calls it weather, calls it nothing,
never once asking what stone is for.

The rocks don't confide in anyone.
They've been pressed under sky
since before there were names for any of this

and what looks like patience

isn't

I thought I was being told a secret.
But I was only standing where two gods
have been arguing for an age
one as touch,
one as ground,

The secret is what's being made beneath it.
going hard and grey in the dark
the way a thing goes hard
when it's being kept for something.

Yes, I thought I was being told a secret, but it was just that I was standing too close to a forge.

She wasn't whispering love beneath the water

she was tempering something,
edge by edge,
teaching stone what it's for,
and the only message that mattered
was: not yet, not yet, not yet...
