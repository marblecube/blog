"777"
by Vidal Palacios

Sprite or 7up? 7up is cheaper and more traditional; conservative. 
But, look the Ginger Ale is what you really wanted. It's Canadian. 

At the Rite-Aid a girl had bright red galoshes. I complimented her
on them as we waited in line. The register didn't work so we had
to switch to a different one.

A different girl had triple sevens across her collarbone. I 
mentioned the Gematria and the number of God.

The girl in the red galoshes owes 7 dollars and 11 cents. 

Did you know that the famous convenience store is so named because
of its original hours of operation? --from seven in the morning
until eleven in the evening.

When I paid and was collecting my change the clerk said, "And
you're change is 7.77." 

We all gawked at the receipt and I decided these were,
collectively, a good sign.
